# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Engineers (and eng leads) running or evaluating a self-hosted CopilotKit runtime. They arrive skeptical of marketing, allergic to gimmicks, and short on time. Context: sent a link in Discord/Slack/an email, deciding in ~10 seconds whether this is a real diagnostic or a lead trap.

## Product Purpose
A self-contained interactive lead asset: 14 questions → readiness score, weakest rung, one thing to fix first. Success = an engineer finishes it, trusts the result, and emails/copies it to their team (the lead event). Everything runs client-side; credibility is the conversion mechanism.

## Brand Personality
CopilotKit official system: calm, technical, precise. Three words: **engineered, light, quietly-delightful**. Playfulness is expressed as craft — satisfying interaction feedback, CI-check metaphors, mono annotations — never mascots, confetti, or jokes. Per brand rules: gradients/glows are ambient support behind content, never the contrast mechanism for text; mono reads as technical annotation, not brand voice; when in doubt, simplify.

## Anti-references
- Marketing-quiz energy (Typeform confetti, emoji scores, "You're a Rockstar!").
- Dark "terminal cosplay" dev-tool landing pages — CopilotKit is a light, editorial surface.
- SaaS gradient-text heroes and hero-metric templates.
- Anything that undermines the credibility pass: no absolute privacy claims, no contradictory labels.

## Design Principles
1. **Credibility is the design.** Every visual choice must survive an engineer's "is this real?" squint. Honest states beat impressive states.
2. **Delight through feedback, not decoration.** The playful budget goes into answering interactions (press states, rungs "passing" like CI checks, the results reveal) — moments that reward engagement.
3. **Mono = annotation.** Spline Sans Mono marks data, counts, and statuses; Plus Jakarta Sans carries voice.
4. **Ambient color, ink text.** Lilac/mint/glow live behind and around content; text is always ink on light.
5. **One file, no dependencies.** Constraint is a feature — the asset must remain trivially portable to CopilotKit's team.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: text ≥4.5:1 (large ≥3:1), visible focus states on all interactive elements, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation, full keyboard operability (all controls are real `<button>`s).
